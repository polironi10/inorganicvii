What the notes cover in Chapter 4, and what is still missing

Covered by your notes: crystalline and amorphous solids, lattice, basis, unit cell, symmetry elements, Bravais lattices, close packing (hcp, ccp), voids, radius ratio rules, AB and AB₂ type structures, perovskite, ilmenite, rutile, tolerance factor and structural distortion, normal and inverse spinel, cation distribution, diamond cubic, zinc blende and wurtzite, point defects (Schottky and Frenkel), F-centres (as a question), n-type and p-type semiconductors (as a margin question only).

Not in your notes yet: types of bonding in solids, lattice energy, Born-Haber cycle, Madelung constant, crystal field considerations in solids, silicates (pyroxenes, amphiboles, talc, mica, clays, zeolites), line and surface defects, non-stoichiometric compounds, defect equilibria, band theory, conductors, insulators and semiconductors, superconductivity, magnetism (dia-, para-, ferro-, antiferro- and ferrimagnetism), polarization, ferroelectricity, piezoelectricity, liquid crystals. Corrections made on the site

  • Ilmenite: Fe2+, not Fe3+.
  • Perovskite A-site list: Cr removed from the AII list.
  • Tetrahedral radius-ratio working: the crossed-out algebra in the notes was not reproduced; the standard limiting value 0.225 is given.
  • Tolerance-factor limits: the standard 0.71 to 1.0 ranges are given, with the notes’ 0.8 / 1.05 rule marked as an approximation.
  • BCC paragraph: the in-plane neighbour count of four is not the bcc coordination number, which is 8.
  • Zinc-blende working: the notes' tetrahedral algebra is crossed out and its last legible step gives 0.449, not 0.225. The page gives the standard working instead, a quarter of the body diagonal, r+ + r− = √3a/4.
  • Tolerance factor: the notes' 0.8 / 1.05 collapse limits are kept as the working rule, and the table now says the ranges are a guide only, because MgTiO3 (t = 0.81) and FeTiO3 (t ≈ 0.82) are ilmenite inside the nominal perovskite range, and CaTiO3 (t ≈ 0.97) is orthorhombic despite t near 1.
  • The notes write “stacking fold”; the standard term is “stacking fault”.
  • Antifluorite (Li2O, Na2O) and the radius-ratio “guide, not a law” sentence are standard material added by the site, not in the notes, and are marked as such on the pages.

Design

Redesigned from scratch to a warm lab-notebook look: paper #FAF7F1, deep teal accent, Fraunces heads over Source Sans 3, one 760px column shared by the text and the figures, built with the Hallmark design skill (nutlope/hallmark, installed with npx skills add nutlope/hallmark -g) and checked against the taste and UI/UX skills. The notes' wording is kept word for word, organised into topics; anything added or corrected is flagged in a note on the page. All figures are inline SVG drawn for this site, no photographs of the handwritten pages are reproduced, so the text is searchable. Figures are animated as an extra, with a pause button in the footer and a still version under reduced-motion settings.
