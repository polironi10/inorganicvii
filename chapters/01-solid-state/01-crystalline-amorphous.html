Crystalline and amorphous solids

Chapter 1 · Solid state and crystal structures

Crystalline

  • Definite, repeating, long range order of particles.
  • Definite characteristic shape.
  • Sharp and fixed melting point.
  • Breaks along a definite plane, giving smooth surfaces.
  • A definite value of heat of fusion.

Examples: NaCl, diamond.

Amorphous

  • Irregular; only short range order.
  • Irregular shape.
  • Softens gradually over a range of temperature.
  • Breaks irregularly.
  • No definite value of heat of fusion.

Examples: glass, rubber, pitch.

Glass is the usual exam example of an amorphous solid: it has no long range order, so it has no sharp melting point.
