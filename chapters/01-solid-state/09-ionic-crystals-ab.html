Ionic crystals of AB type

Chapter 1 · Solid state and crystal structures

AB type, four structures

NaCl, CsCl, ZnS (zinc blende) and ZnS (wurtzite).

StructureCoordinationAnion arrayCation site
NaCl (rock salt)6:6fcc Cl−Na+ in all octahedral holes; 4 Na+ and 4 Cl− per cell
CsCl8:8Simple cubic Cl−Cs+ at the body centre; 1 of each per cell
ZnS, zinc blende4:4fcc S2−Zn2+ in half the tetrahedral holes; 4 of each per cell
ZnS, wurtzite4:4hcp S2−Zn2+ in half the tetrahedral holes

Zinc blende and wurtzite have the same 4:4 coordination and the same stoichiometry; they differ in the stacking of the anion array, ccp in zinc blende, hcp in wurtzite.
